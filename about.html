Our story

We believe a repair visit can feel different.

Fixora began with a familiar frustration: when an appliance breaks, it can be hard to know who to call, what the repair will cost, or whether anyone will arrive on time.

We set out to make that experience more human and straightforward. One convenient booking connects households with skilled local technicians, clear estimates and support that stays with you after the repair.

Today, we help Mumbai households care for the appliances that keep everyday life moving—from the first diagnosis to the final check.
