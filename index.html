Common questions

A few things worth knowing

Still unsure about something? Our team is happy to explain the visit, the quote or the warranty before you book.

Contact us Call Now
How much does a repair visit cost?

The diagnostic visit is ₹249. If you approve the repair quote, that visit charge is waived, so you only pay for the approved parts and labour.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Yes, subject to technician availability. Book before 6 PM and we will aim for a same-day slot. Most requests get a response within 60 minutes.

Is the quote final before work starts?

Yes. The technician diagnoses the fault and shares the repair cost first. You can approve, ask questions or decline before any repair work begins.

What does the 30-day warranty cover?

It covers our workmanship for 30 days. If the same fault returns, we come back and fix it free. Replacement parts also carry their manufacturer warranty.

Which areas do you serve?

We cover Mumbai and nearby areas. Share your pin code when booking and we will confirm the nearest available technician and time slot.
