FAQ

Common questions

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. When you approve the repair estimate, the diagnostic visit fee is completely waived. You only start paying when work begins.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently serve Mumbai and nearby areas including Andheri, Powai, Goregaon, Bandra and Malad. Enter your address at booking to confirm coverage.

What happens if the same problem returns?

Every repair includes a 30-day workmanship warranty. If the same fault reappears within the period, we come back and fix it free of charge.

Do you use genuine spare parts?

Yes — genuine or equivalent-grade parts sourced for your appliance model, fitted and tested on-site. Old parts are handed back to you.

Is this a real booking service?

No. Fixora is a demonstration website. Bookings, tracking, payments and admin flows are simulated entirely in your browser.
