Pricing questions, answered

How much is the service visit?

The standard visit and diagnostic charge is ₹249. It covers travel, on-site fault finding and a written quote. The charge is waived when you approve the repair; if you decline, you pay only ₹249.

Will the final price ever exceed the quote?

You approve the quote before work starts. If a part turns out to be costlier once the appliance is opened, we stop and confirm the revised price with you — never surprise billing.

Is labour included in the estimated repair cost?

Yes. The repair ranges shown on this page include standard labour for that job. Any additional parts are listed separately in your quote and approved by you before fitting.

Are spare parts genuine?

Genuine or equivalent-grade parts are sourced for your appliance model and fitted on-site. The replaced old part is returned to you.

What if a part has to be ordered?

The technician confirms the part price and expected arrival time first. The visit charge is waived when you approve the repair, and the follow-up fitting visit is included in the agreed quote.

How can I pay for the repair?

In this demo, payments are simulated. In a real service you would pay by cash or UPI after the repair is tested and you are satisfied with the work.
