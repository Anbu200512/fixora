Important note

All prices are illustrative sample estimates for this demo website. Actual charges depend on the appliance model, fault complexity, and genuine spare parts required. This is not a real price list.

Is the diagnostic visit really free?

Yes. When you approve the repair estimate, the diagnostic visit fee is waived. If you choose not to proceed, the standard diagnostic fee applies.

Will the final price ever exceed the quote?

You approve the quote before work starts. If parts turn out costlier during the repair, we stop and confirm with you again — never surprise billing.

Are spare parts genuine?

Genuine or equivalent-grade parts are sourced for your appliance model and fitted on-site. The replaced old part is returned to you.
