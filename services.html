How is the visit charge calculated?

The standard visit charge is ₹249 and covers travel, on-site diagnosis and a written quote. It is waived completely when you approve the repair, so you only pay for the work you agree to.

Can ovens and dishwashers be repaired at home?

Most built-in ovens, OTGs, convection ovens and dishwashers are diagnosed and repaired at home. If a part needs workshop-level service, the technician explains the timeline and cost before taking it away.

How long does a typical repair take?

Most repairs are completed in 45–90 minutes once approved. Jobs that need a special-order part are scheduled for a follow-up visit, and you approve the part cost first.

Are the spare parts genuine?

Yes. We fit genuine or equivalent-grade parts matched to your appliance model. Every repair includes a 30-day workmanship warranty, and replaced parts are handed back to you.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us the appliance and the symptom. We will point you to the right repair and confirm the visit charge upfront.
